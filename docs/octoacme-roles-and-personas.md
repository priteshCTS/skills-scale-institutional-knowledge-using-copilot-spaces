# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Manager / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Manager or Delivery Lead ensures the engineering team has clear direction, capacity, and support to deliver work reliably. They connect technical execution with project planning and cross-functional coordination.

### Responsibilities
- Align engineering priorities with roadmap milestones and sprint goals
- Manage team capacity, staffing, and workload balancing
- Help resolve technical blockers, dependencies, and delivery risks
- Support quality, engineering standards, and continuous improvement
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on sequencing and trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver predictable, sustainable engineering execution
- Maintain healthy team throughput and quality
- Improve visibility into delivery health and constraints

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering planning and delivery reviews
- Dependency discussions with PMs and product leads
- Escalation updates for staffing, technical risks, or delivery shortfalls

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to support execution and remove blockers
- Collaborates with Project Managers on timeline, risk, and resourcing trade-offs
- Aligns with Product Managers on feature sequencing and scope decisions
- Keeps stakeholders informed on delivery readiness and risk exposure

---

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance partners review programs, features, and release plans for risk, governance, and policy alignment. They help teams build secure products without slowing down delivery unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements and implementation decisions for security and compliance risks
- Define or validate controls for data handling, access, privacy, and platform safety
- Support security reviews, audits, and remediation planning
- Assess release and deployment readiness from a governance perspective
- Partner with engineering and leadership on policy exceptions and mitigation plans

### Goals
- Reduce security, privacy, and compliance risk
- Support secure-by-default delivery practices
- Enable informed decisions without unnecessary friction

### Typical Communication
- Design and architecture reviews
- Risk discussions during planning and release readiness
- Security incident and remediation coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure implementation patterns and controls
- Partners with Project Managers and Product Managers on risk tracking and go/no-go decisions
- Escalates serious issues to leadership and stakeholders when risk thresholds are reached
- Provides stakeholder-facing guidance for compliance commitments and operational readiness

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
The Data / Analytics Lead defines how project outcomes are measured, tracked, and validated. They make sure teams have the instrumentation and reporting needed to understand impact and improve decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, product measures, and reporting needs
- Identify instrumentation, dashboards, and data quality requirements
- Validate whether planned changes are producing expected customer or operational outcomes
- Partner with stakeholders on analysis and decision-making based on evidence
- Translate metrics into actionable insights for roadmap and release decisions

### Goals
- Make outcomes measurable and visible
- Improve learning cycles through data-informed decisions
- Ensure product changes are evaluated against real business value

### Typical Communication
- KPI and dashboard reviews
- Milestone and release impact analysis
- User and business outcome discussions with Product and Stakeholder groups

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define goals and success metrics
- Supports Developers and QA in validating data quality and event correctness
- Shares readiness signals with Project Managers for milestone progress and launch decisions
- Provides objective evidence to stakeholders for business impact assessment

---

## Customer Success / Support Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support representatives provide direct insight from real-world customer usage, escalations, and operational pain points. They translate frontline feedback into product and process improvement opportunities.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer friction, bugs, and unmet needs
- Share support trends and impact from product issues or outages
- Help prioritize workflow and quality improvements based on user experience
- Provide context for release readiness and rollout communication
- Partner with product and delivery teams on service reliability and customer communication

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce friction in product adoption and support interactions
- Turn field feedback into actionable product and process improvements

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and service health discussions
- Support escalation and issue triage updates
- Stakeholder briefings on friction points and usage trends

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with customer context for prioritization
- Shares issue patterns with Developers and QA to guide fixes and validation
- Informs Project Managers about service risks or release concerns impacting customer trust
- Helps stakeholders understand operational impact and customer sentiment

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads ensure that the team validates outcomes against acceptance criteria, quality standards, and release readiness requirements. They coordinate testing strategy and defect triage across the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality gates for feature readiness and release validation
- Review acceptance criteria and test coverage for new work
- Coordinate functional, integration, and regression testing activities
- Track defects, risk areas, and release blockers
- Partner with engineering and product teams to confirm readiness for sign-off

### Goals
- Reduce defects escaping into release
- Improve confidence in product quality and user experience
- Make the definition of done visible and measurable

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and defect review meetings
- Release readiness checkpoints
- Go/no-go discussions with PMs, Product, and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate fixes and quality expectations
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and user outcomes
- Supports Project Managers with milestone readiness and release risk assessment
- Escalates high-risk defects or open issues that affect delivery confidence

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These extended roles support clearer accountability, improve cross-functional communication, and make project execution easier to scale across teams.
